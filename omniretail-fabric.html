Case Study 04 · Microsoft Fabric

OmniRetail Analytics Platform

Overview

The OmniRetail Analytics Platform is an end-to-end analytics solution built on Microsoft Fabric to consolidate sales, inventory, and customer data from multiple retail channels into a unified reporting platform. The project demonstrates modern analytics engineering practices using Fabric's Lakehouse architecture, transforming raw retail data into trusted business metrics that support operational and executive decision-making.

Business Challenge

Retail businesses generate data from multiple channels, including physical stores, e-commerce platforms, and mobile applications. Without a unified analytics platform, reporting becomes fragmented, making it difficult to monitor sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behaviour across the business.

Solution

I designed a modern analytics platform in Microsoft Fabric, ingesting raw retail data into a Lakehouse before transforming it through Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers. Curated datasets were then used to power interactive Power BI dashboards, providing a single source of truth for business reporting.
