Case Study 02 · SaaS Analytics

CryptoNex SaaS Analytics Platform

Overview

CryptoNex is a cloud-based analytics platform designed to transform raw SaaS subscription data into business-ready insights using Snowflake's Medallion Architecture. The project focuses on recurring revenue analytics, subscription performance, churn analysis, and executive reporting.

Business Challenge

SaaS companies rely on recurring revenue metrics to monitor business performance. Without a structured warehouse, calculating metrics such as MRR, ARR, and customer churn becomes difficult and inconsistent.

Solution

I designed a modern ELT pipeline within Snowflake using Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers. Business metrics are generated directly from curated Gold tables and presented through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
